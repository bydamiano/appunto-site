Your meetings, remembered. Privately.

Appunto is a meeting recorder for macOS, Windows and Linux. It records any call (Google Meet, Zoom, Teams, …) straight from your computer, transcribes it on your computer, and turns it into notes, decisions and to-dos you can search. No bot joins your calls.

No bots, local transcripts

Audio is transcribed on your computer and never leaves it. English, Italian and ten more languages.

✅

To-dos

Every action item heard in a meeting becomes a to-do, with a link back to the moment it was said.

💬

Ask your meetings

“What did we decide about PROJ-142?” Answers quote the exact moment, with the AI provider you choose.

Your tools, your AI

Jira, GitHub, GitLab, Azure DevOps and Google Calendar, plus Claude or Codex through MCP. All optional.

What leaves your computer

Nothing, unless you turn on a feature that needs it. Read exactly what is sent, to whom, and what is stored in the Privacy Policy.
